# ABC Boutique Footwear — fictional draft policy

Standard products may be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to condition inspection.
Commission is paid seven days after delivery. Returned orders should not earn commission.
The mechanism for recovering commission after payout is not documented.
An additional coupon's eligibility is not documented.
If two affiliates claim an order, the team currently resolves it manually without a written tie-breaker.
Worn or damaged footwear: condition exceptions require owner review.
A cheaper exchange may require a commission adjustment; the rule is still undecided.
